AI-Powered Brand Experience / Coffee & Beverage MVP

Roasté

An immersive coffee experience that takes visitors on a journey from bean to cup. The prototype combines storytelling, rich visuals, and AI-powered interactions to help coffee lovers discover where their coffee comes from, how it is made, and what type of coffee suits their taste.

Key features
  • From Farm to Cup — Explore the journey of coffee, from growing and harvesting to roasting and brewing.
  • Brewing Experience — Discover different brewing methods and how they influence the final cup.
  • AI-Powered Coffee Discovery — An interactive experience that uses AI to help visitors explore coffee based on their preferences.
  • Find Your Coffee Quiz — A fun quiz that learns about your taste preferences and suggests the type of coffee that may suit you.
  • Interactive Storytelling — Educational and engaging content that brings the coffee-making journey to life.
  • Responsive Experience — Designed to provide a seamless experience across desktop, tablet, and mobile.
Goal
To create an engaging digital experience that educates, entertains, and helps customers discover coffee—while demonstrating how AI and interactive storytelling can turn a traditional product into a memorable digital brand experience.
