Meet Our Founder & CEO

Arun MandookPMP | Salesforce 3x Certified

Arun Mandook is the Founder and CEO of IgnizAI, bringing a strong blend of technology, product, consulting, and business experience to the company.

Before founding IgnizAI, Arun served as Associate Vice President – Technology & Product at The House of Abhinandan Lodha, a pan-India real estate brand. In this role, he worked at the intersection of technology, product strategy, and business, gaining valuable experience in building and scaling digital solutions within a highly competitive industry.

With extensive experience across technology, consulting, and real estate, Arun brings a practical understanding of how businesses can use technology not just to operate better, but to build stronger brands, improve customer experiences, and create meaningful digital presence.

At IgnizAI, his vision is simple: help genuine businesses become more visible, credible, and trusted through the right combination of technology, design, and storytelling.
