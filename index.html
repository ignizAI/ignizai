Questions

Good to know.

Still unsure? Ask us directly or message us on WhatsApp.

How much does a website cost?

Our packages start at AED 6,250 for Ignite Basic, AED 18,050 for Ignite Business and AED 45,250 for Ignite Premium. Ignite Intelligence — bookings, payments, CRM and AI — is AED 22,100. Domain and hosting may be billed at the provider's current market rate.

Can my website be in both English and Arabic?

Yes. Ignite Premium is a bilingual site and includes English to Arabic translation, and Ignite Intelligence can add a bilingual chatbot.

Can you add AI to the business I already have?

Yes. Ignite Intelligence adds AI to your website — an AI chatbot for FAQs, lead qualification, booking, payments and CRM integration.

What happens after launch?

Launch is the beginning. With Care & Growth we handle maintenance and updates, uptime and performance monitoring, SEO and priority support.
